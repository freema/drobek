# Code of Conduct

drobek adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep
discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
tolerated in issues, pull requests, Discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@freema](https://github.com/freema). Reports are handled confidentially; the
maintainer may remove content and restrict or ban participants who break
this code.
